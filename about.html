About Curtis

My name is Curtis Burke. I am married with twin toddlers and live in Los Angeles, CA. I was born in Charleston, SC—known as the Low Country—and moved to Atlanta as a young teenager. I graduated from Tennessee State University with a bachelor’s degree in speech communication and theater, then earned a master’s degree in human development and social change from Pacific Oaks College.

While in graduate school, I became deeply interested in how humans grow, change, and socially evolve. After taking an ancestry DNA test, I experienced firsthand the limitations many Black Americans face when learning about our backgrounds. Investigating my own family legacy inspired me to start Uncovered Legacy.

The podcast gives ordinary people from a wide range of backgrounds a platform to preserve their stories, celebrate the people around them, and maintain the memory of their ancestors.
